# Voiceover script — consensus_overview.mp4 (~70s)

The narration never reads the on-screen text — the viewer can read it. Each
cue adds context on top of what is visible: framing, intuition, stakes.
Start each cue when its block's text lands. Target pace ~150 wpm.
Pronunciation: say "lambda" for λ.

## [0:00–0:06] Title card (subtitle ~0:03)

Campaigns obsess over winning. We asked what happens after — once the winner
controls the money.

## [0:06–0:14] The question (line two ~0:09, line three ~0:12)

That sounds like a question about character. It isn't — same voters, same
candidates. The only thing we change is the election rule.

## [0:14–0:25] The setup (voters ~0:15, candidates ~0:18, budget ~0:21)

Picture a divided town. Some of these candidates would govern for everyone;
others, only for their own side — and nothing on the ballot tells you which
is which.

## [0:25–0:35] The rule (formula ~0:27, ballot line ~0:31)

Lambda is loyalty. A loyal winner funnels the directed 72% of the budget toward the projects their voters love; a fair one points that share at everyone. Either way, the platform captures 28%. Voters are choosing blind.

## [0:35–0:43] The contenders (cards land 0:36–0:39)

Four rules, all perfectly reasonable on paper. The real difference is hidden:
each one builds the winner a different coalition.

## [0:43–1:01] The result (bars 0:45–0:48, chip ~0:48, loyalty ~0:52, twist ~0:56)

Watch the minority bloc — the same people under every rule. Election
"losers" are a different set each time, so they are not the contrast.
(beat) Default cell: voters never see lambda, so a flat loyalty profile
is construction, not a finding.
(beat) Appendix: if platforms hint at loyalty, some rules do pick
lower-lambda winners.

## [1:01–1:10] Takeaway (line two ~1:03, credit ~1:05)

That's the lesson: institutions beat intentions. If you want losers treated
well, don't hunt for better politicians — change how you choose them.

---

Direction: even, curious tone; let the on-screen text carry the facts while
the read carries the meaning. Hold the two (beat) pauses in the result block
until the loyalty line and the twist line fade in — the animation leaves
three seconds after each. If a read runs long, stretch the `self.wait` calls
in `farm/experiments/consensus/overview_video.py` rather than rushing.
